KAMMO — A STORY WRITTEN IN STARS
================================

1. Open index.html in a browser. Everything works offline except Google Fonts.
2. All 18 photos are included in assets/memory1.jpg through memory18.jpg.
3. The original animated 12-second video is in assets/our-story.mp4. It is included and plays with the built-in video player.
4. Optional music: add your own MP3 as assets/our-song.mp3. The music control only plays after a click.
5. Games: memory matching, 3x3 photo puzzle, story quiz, and heart-catching. Completing all four unlocks the Jyotisar invitation.
6. To share a URL, upload the CONTENTS of this folder (index.html and assets/) to Netlify Drop or another static host.
7. This website contains personal photos. Share privately and obtain consent before publishing photos publicly.
8. Jyotisar answer appears on her screen only; the site does not send her response to you.
